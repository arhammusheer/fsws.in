# Client marks

Third-party trademarks, shown as customer references. Each is the owner's own
mark, unaltered in shape or colour: the only processing is cropping, removing a
white background, and trimming to the ink.

The wall renders the `-light` files. The `-dark` files predate this build and are
not referenced by anything; they came across with the rest of `public/assets`.

## Provenance

| Mark | Source | Processing |
|---|---|---|
| ITC, Unilever, Wipro, BHEL, EFS | carried over from the previous fsws.in build | none |
| SKF | [`SKF-Logo.svg`](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:SKF-Logo.svg), Wikimedia Commons | rasterised from vector at 1200px wide, trimmed |
| Themis Medicare | back cover of the [Themis Medicare annual report 2024-25](http://www.themismedicare.com/uploads/statutory/pdf/Themis_AR_-_2024-2025_9.pdf) | cropped, white unmultiplied to alpha, "QUALITY YOU CAN TRUST" strapline dropped |

Two things to know if you replace one of these.

**Check the company before you take a mark.** "SKF" also returns SK+F Eskayef
Pharmaceuticals of Bangladesh, whose logo is a completely different blue
wordmark. The customer here is SKF India, the bearings company.

**Resolution.** The wall displays marks at 64px tall, so anything under about
130px tall in the source will look soft on a retina screen. That ruled out the
120x57 logo on themismedicare.com and sent this to the annual report instead.
Company PDFs are usually the best public source: annual reports, catalogues and
letterheads often carry the mark as vector or at print resolution.
